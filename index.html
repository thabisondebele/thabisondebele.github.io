// About Me

"""Hello! I am Thabiso, a data scientist who turns raw, messy data into models and insights that actually mean something."""
"""Based in Johannesburg, my journey into data science was sparked by a fascination with how Formula 1 engineers use analytics to find competitive advantages. Watching race strategy unfold in real time through data, from tyre degradation models to lap time predictions, made me realize that data science is where curiosity meets real world impact. I now bring that same obsession with patterns and performance to every dataset I work with."""
"""There are three things I enjoy the most: fast cars, a machine learning model that converges on the first run, and a prediction problem that refuses to cooperate. So if your data needs structure, your features need engineering, or your model needs tuning, let me take a look. We will figure it out."""
